Oinkdoku Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Oinkdoku is a puzzle game that respects your privacy. The short version: we don't collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Oinkdoku has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, statistics, Daily Pen streak and settings) is saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It is included in your device's own backups if you have them turned on. Nobody else, including us, can see it.

Sharing

If you tap Share after solving a Daily Pen, the game opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text summary of your result. You choose whether and where to send it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or delete the app.

Children

Oinkdoku is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Visit the Oinkdoku support page.
